# INK 1.0 readiness evidence

This document records the evidence for an eventual INK 1.0 that does **not**
depend on the production soak now in progress. It is a running record, not a
readiness claim. The soak-dependent parts (a release-candidate readiness
report, a soak-evidence summary and any promotion recommendation) are deferred
until the soak window closes and are listed at the end.

The current state is: soak-independent evidence recorded; production-soak
evidence pending; no 1.0 readiness claim yet.

This file is maintainer governance material and is intentionally kept outside
the published package (it is not in `package.json` `files`).

## 1. Conformance corpus attestation

The cross-implementation conformance corpus is the authoritative wire contract
for the current version. It is exercised by the TypeScript reference and by an
independent Go verifier, which must reach the same accept or reject decision on
every vector.

- Corpus id: `ink.conformance.v1`
- Manifest format: `ink.conformance.manifest.v1`
- Coverage: 20 categories, 435 vectors
- Manifest integrity anchor (SHA-256 of `conformance/v1/manifest.json`):
  `81a3623f4f892636aa04b4f165aaad3c7250a4dcebf4c5cb15e1dc24a9e5d32d`

The manifest carries a per-category SHA-256 of each vector file, and the
manifest-integrity suite asserts that the on-disk vectors, the declared
category set and every per-category digest match in both implementations. A
verifier therefore cannot silently drift from the corpus without the digest
changing.

Reference results, recorded against release commit `8a5c585` (the `0.9.0`
release):

- TypeScript reference: the conformance and manifest-integrity suites pass
  (561 test cases across the two suites), covering the full 435-vector corpus
  plus the integrity cross-checks.
- Independent Go verifier: runs the same corpus and the same manifest-integrity
  cross-check in the `go-conformance` CI job, which is green on `8a5c585`. Both
  implementations agree on the category set and on every vector's decision.

## 2. Frozen wire contract

- The mandatory base profile (the sender and receiver MUST set) is frozen. It
  is the 13 `base`-profile categories in the manifest, covering envelope
  structure, JCS number safety, signed-string surrogate safety, raw-body UTF-8
  validity, the Ed25519 signing base, Agent Card schema and discovery fetch, the
  connection and first-contact handshake payloads, key rotation authority,
  timestamp grammar, replay freshness, principal normalization and the
  private-hostname SSRF guard.
- The vendor-neutral `network.ink.*` message namespace is the candidate 1.0
  wire namespace, under evaluation through the soak. Receivers dual-accept it
  alongside the legacy `network.tulpa.*` spelling, receiver-first and backward
  compatible, as specified in `specs/ink-compatibility-policy.md`. The two
  audit types whose detached signatures do not cover the envelope type stay
  single-spelling and are documented as such.
- Optional capability profiles ship as separately versioned extensions, not as
  1.0 base requirements: the `audit`, `witness`, `containment`, `encryption`,
  `discovery` and `authorization` profiles. The encrypted-payload (ECIES)
  envelope is an optional
  `encryption`-profile extension and is explicitly out of the 1.0 core. Its AAD
  composition is therefore not part of the frozen base contract and may still
  evolve, consistent with its extension status.

## 4. Deferred until the soak closes

The following are soak-dependent and are intentionally **not** asserted here:

- A 1.0 release-candidate readiness report.
- A soak-evidence summary (duration met, error-rate and compatibility
  observations, incident-free or incident-reviewed).
- A recommendation to promote `next` to `latest` or to cut a 1.0 line.

Soak exit criteria, recorded so the eventual summary has a fixed bar to meet:

- A production integrator runs the release under evaluation on the `next`
  dist-tag (currently `0.10.0`) against real inbound and outbound traffic for the
  full soak window with no signature, namespace or encrypted-path regressions.
- No rise in envelope-rejection or transport-auth-failure rates attributable
  to the release.
- The conformance corpus and the `go-conformance` CI job remain green on the
  release commit for the duration.

When those hold, the deferred items above may be written and the result may be
considered by the human promotion gate. Meeting the criteria is necessary, not
automatically sufficient.
